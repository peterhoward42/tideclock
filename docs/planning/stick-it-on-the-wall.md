## The idea

The tide dial is designed deliberately to work well if you "stick it on the wall". Perhaps on a tablet that lives in your kitchen, or a wall mounted screen that people walk past.

There's a dedicated <strong>install<option> to make the best of that choice.

It will then fill the screen completely, and most importantly keep the screen awake.
