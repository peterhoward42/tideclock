 ## Cookie policy

 The Tide Dial uses only necessary cookies.
 Specifically:
 - We do not discover, nor ever know your identity, and do not store it in any form.
 - We do fetch the required tide information over the internet and the app cannot function without doing so.
 - We do store the location you have chosen to show tides for - but only on your own computer. This avoids you having to choose it every time you visit The Tide Dial.
