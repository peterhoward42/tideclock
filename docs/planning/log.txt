The Tide Dial is the result of a recipe made up with ingredients drawn from my
career, my life interests and enthusiathasms, and me moving to live in a place
much influenced by tides.

The bridge spanning the river I cross to leave my house at certain tide times
is completely underwater. So I go there to Kayak. At other times it is clear -
so I drive over it to pop out for a pint of milk. Choosing the wrong time for
either activity is doomed.

If you are unused to tides they can be a source of delight and interest if you
visit a tidal place. Tides bring colour and an inexorable rythm to life. The
fishing fleet returning to port, not when they please, but when the tide is
high. People letting their dogs have fun charging around the exposed shore at
low tides. You'll notice the tide times creeping forward by just under an hour
each day. You'll wonder why some are huge and some are tame. Fascinating to
some.

You can look up tide times quite easily but you have to make the effort,
sometimes wait for an advert they make you watch first, and then you just see
numbers. If you want to know how long it is before the bridge will be open
again - you have to start doing mental arithmetic.

Most of my 40 year career as a software engineer has been about bringing alive
information through diagrams - engineering design systems, physics
simulations, the circuit inside a computer chip, and a visualisation of how
you mix and dilute chemicals with microscropic accuracy for your reasearch
into DNA. 

I can't myself - I have always created software because I enjoy it so much.
Until recently people employed me or contracted me to do it. But now I'm semi
retired I'm still at it - because it's still fun. But now I can make software
systems to please me. The Tide Dial is exactly that. I rather hope it will
please you too.

Feel free to get in touch if you're interested in the code behind The Tide
Dial - I'd enjoy that - and in some situations might be useful to you.
